Frequently Asked Questions

Is AgriHealth AI free to use?

Yes. You can check your plants for free without creating an account. The website is supported by ads.

Can the AI replace an agriculture officer?

No. The AI gives you a quick first opinion so you know what to look for and who to ask. For serious, spreading or urgent problems, always contact an expert. We never suggest spray or medicine doses.

Which crops can I check?

19 crops, including wheat, rice, cotton, maize, sugarcane, vegetables, citrus, mango, guava and date palm. Symptom checks work for all of them. Photo checks work for 16 crops (all except chickpea, mustard and okra), and more plants are being added.

Do you keep my photos?

No. Photos are only used to check for disease and are not stored. Read our privacy policy for details.

What kind of photo works best?

A close, sharp photo in daylight where the sick part of the leaf fills most of the picture. See our photo tips.
