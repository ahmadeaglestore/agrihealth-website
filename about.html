About Us

We are on a mission to help farmers and gardeners spot plant disease early, using simple and honest AI.

Our mission

Early help for the people who feed us

Every season, crops are lost because a problem was noticed too late, or because expert help was far away. AgriHealth AI gives farmers and gardeners a quick, free first opinion, so they know what they may be facing and when to call an expert.

AgriHealth AI is built by Your Name, a student at Your University, as a final-year machine learning project that we plan to keep improving after graduation.

Why choose us

Built to Be Useful and Safe

AI-Powered

Easy to Use

Trusted Sources

Our guides are based on FAO, university extension services and plant protection references.

Safety First

Technology

How Our AI Works

The detection tool uses small machine learning models, each with one job. On 14,651 test photos it never saw while learning, the photo check gave the right answer about 95% of the time. Real field photos can be harder, so we keep testing and improving it.

Leaf disease classifier

Live

Image model (CNN, transfer learning)

Looks at a leaf photo and ranks the most likely diseases for the crop you chose.

Photo check

Live

Out-of-distribution detection

Checks whether a photo really shows a plant before naming a disease.

Heatmap (Grad-CAM)

Live

Explainable AI

Highlights the part of the photo that most influenced the result.

Symptom matcher

Live

Text embeddings

Compares the symptoms you describe with our disease library and asks a follow-up question when unsure.

Honest limits: AI can make mistakes, especially with blurry photos, unusual varieties or diseases that look alike. That is why we always show other possibilities and tell you when to contact an expert. Read more in How AI Detects Plant Disease from a Photo.
