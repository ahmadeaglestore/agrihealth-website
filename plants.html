Prevention

Good Habits That Prevent Crop Disease

Rotate your crops

Avoid growing the same crop in the same field every season. Many diseases survive in old plant debris.

Use healthy seed

Certified seed and resistant varieties are the cheapest protection against disease.

Water the soil, not the leaves

Wet leaves help fungi and bacteria spread. Water at the base of plants where possible.

Scout every week

Walk through your field regularly and check both sides of the leaves.
