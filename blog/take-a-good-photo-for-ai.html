How to Take a Photo the AI Can Read

By the AgriHealth AI Team · September 27, 2026 · 4 min read

An AI model can only judge what it can see. A blurry photo taken from far away hides the very details that tell one disease from another. A few seconds of care when taking the photo gives you a much more useful result.

For plants

  • One leaf, close up: pick the leaf that shows the problem most clearly and let it fill most of the photo.
  • Daylight, no flash: soft daylight shows true colours. Avoid strong shadows across the leaf.
  • Plain background: holding the leaf against the sky, the ground or your palm helps the AI focus on it.
  • Sharp focus: tap the leaf on your phone screen to focus, and hold still.
  • Show the symptom: make sure the spots, powder or discoloured area are clearly visible.

When a photo is not enough

  • Some problems, such as wilting of the whole plant, rotting roots or damage inside the stem, are hard to see on one leaf. For these, describing the symptoms works better.
  • If the problem is on the fruit or stem, photograph that part close up instead of a leaf.

Common mistakes

  • Photographing the whole field or the whole plant from far away
  • Photos taken at night or in deep shade
  • Several different leaves mixed in one photo
  • Screenshots or photos of a screen

Your privacy

Before uploading, our website shrinks your photo and removes hidden information such as the location where it was taken. Photos are used only to check for disease and are not kept. Read more in our privacy policy.
