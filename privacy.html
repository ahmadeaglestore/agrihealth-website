Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information AgriHealth AI ("we", "us") collects when you use www.yourdomain.com (the "website"), how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Photos and symptom descriptions

When you use the AI detection tool, your photo is resized on your own device before it is checked, which also removes hidden information such as the location where the photo was taken. Photos and descriptions are used only to produce your result and are not stored by us. We do not sell or share them.

A short list of your recent results (disease name and date, never the photo) is saved only in your own browser so you can see it again. You can delete it at any time with the "Clear history" button.

2. Contact messages

If you contact us, we receive your name, email address and message. We use them only to reply to you and do not add you to any mailing list.

3. Information collected automatically

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can learn more about how Google uses information in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Where required by law, for example for visitors in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, we will ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown.

5. Third-party services

These services may receive your IP address when your browser loads their content.

6. Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
